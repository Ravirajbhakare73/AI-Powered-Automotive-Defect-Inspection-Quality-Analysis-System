# Surface Contamination

## Quality Context

Foreign material or contamination can contribute to
visible surface-quality defects.

## Potential Indicators

- Defect appears after surface preparation
- Similar defects occur in a localized process area
- Foreign material appears around affected areas

## Investigation

Inspect:

- Surface cleanliness
- Work area
- Cleaning process
- Tools
- Fixtures
- Protective materials

## Corrective Actions

- Clean affected surfaces
- Inspect cleaning procedure
- Identify contamination source
- Improve material protection
- Monitor recurrence
