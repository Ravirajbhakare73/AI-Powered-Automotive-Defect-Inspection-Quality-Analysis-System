# Crack Defect

## Defect

A crack is a visible fracture or separation on an
automotive component or surface.

## Potential Causes

- Mechanical stress
- Handling impact
- Assembly interference
- Material weakness
- Tool contact
- Process variation

## Diagnostic Investigation

Inspect:

- Crack location
- Crack length
- Crack direction
- Surrounding deformation
- Contact points
- Assembly interference
- Repeated occurrences

## Corrective Actions

- Inspect component condition
- Check assembly interference
- Inspect tooling
- Inspect fixtures
- Review process conditions
- Monitor recurrence

## Root Cause

Computer vision identifies the visible crack.

Engineering and quality investigation are required
to confirm the actual root cause.
