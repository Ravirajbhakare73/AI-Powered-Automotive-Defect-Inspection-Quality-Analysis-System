# Fixture Contact

## Quality Context

Unintended contact between a component and fixture
or tooling surface can produce defects such as
scratches or dents.

## Potential Indicators

- Defect repeatedly appears at the same location
- Damage geometry corresponds to a fixture surface
- Defect appears after a specific operation
- Similar defects occur across multiple units

## Investigation

Inspect:

- Fixture surface
- Fixture alignment
- Contact points
- Component clearance
- Fixture wear
- Part positioning

## Corrective Actions

- Inspect fixture condition
- Correct alignment
- Restore required clearance
- Repair damaged contact surfaces
- Verify the process after correction

## Root Cause

Fixture contact is a potential cause.

It must be confirmed through physical and
process investigation.
