# Automotive Inspection Guidelines

## Inspection Workflow

When a visual defect is detected:

1. Identify the defect type.
2. Verify detection confidence.
3. Inspect the physical defect.
4. Identify the defect location.
5. Review nearby tools and fixtures.
6. Review the preceding process.
7. Check whether the defect is recurring.
8. Investigate potential causes.
9. Apply corrective action.
10. Verify that the defect does not recur.

## Important

Computer vision identifies visible defects.

The system should support quality investigation
rather than replace engineering or physical
root-cause analysis.
