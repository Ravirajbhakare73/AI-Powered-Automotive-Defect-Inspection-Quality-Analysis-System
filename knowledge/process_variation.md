# Process Variation

## Quality Context

Changes in a manufacturing process can increase the
frequency of recurring quality defects.

## Potential Indicators

- Defect frequency increases
- Similar defects occur across multiple units
- Defects become concentrated in a process
- Defect characteristics change over time

## Investigation

Review:

- Process parameters
- Equipment condition
- Tooling
- Fixtures
- Material condition
- Recent process changes

## Corrective Actions

- Identify changing process factors
- Verify equipment condition
- Inspect tooling
- Inspect fixtures
- Review process changes
- Monitor defect recurrence
