# Corrective Actions

## General Approach

Corrective action should be based on investigation
of the observed defect and its potential process causes.

## Possible Actions

- Inspect fixtures
- Inspect tooling
- Verify component clearance
- Review handling
- Check surface cleanliness
- Inspect equipment condition
- Review process parameters
- Verify material condition
- Monitor recurrence

## Verification

After corrective action:

1. Reinspect the process.
2. Verify the affected component.
3. Monitor subsequent units.
4. Check whether the defect recurs.

## Important

AI recommendations should support quality and
engineering decisions.

They should not replace physical verification.
