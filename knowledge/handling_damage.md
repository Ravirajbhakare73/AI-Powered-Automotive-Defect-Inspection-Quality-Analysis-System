# Handling Damage

## Quality Context

Improper handling of components can produce scratches,
dents, or other visible damage.

## Potential Indicators

- Damage appears between handling operations
- Damage occurs on exposed surfaces
- Damage corresponds to handling points
- Similar damage appears across multiple units

## Investigation

Check:

- Handling sequence
- Operator contact points
- Protective materials
- Transport fixtures
- Component stacking
- Contact surfaces

## Corrective Actions

- Review handling procedure
- Improve component protection
- Inspect handling equipment
- Improve operator guidance
- Monitor recurrence
